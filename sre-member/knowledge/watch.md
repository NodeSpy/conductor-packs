# Watch list

What your proactive sweeps should look at beyond the charter's checklist.
Keep it short and current: add an item when an incident shows a blind spot,
remove it when it stops mattering. Each item: what to look at, through which
verb, and what "wrong" looks like.

- (example) `db-prod-1` replica lag — `aws.describe_*` / your metrics verb —
  wrong above 30s for 10 minutes.
